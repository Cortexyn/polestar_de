**Polestar Client** is a small desktop app that keeps Polestar's addons and the community guides from [polestar.guide](https://polestar.guide) installed and up to date, so you never unzip a folder into AddOns by hand again. No account, no sign-in, nothing else on your PC touched.

This is the first release: a beta. If something's off, tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/vMbee6F2e9) or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar/issues).

**First launch:**
- **Windows** may say "Windows protected your PC", because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** the app is signed and checked by Apple; open the .dmg, drag Polestar Client into Applications, and open it.
- **Linux AppImage:** make it executable (right-click > Properties > Allow executing, or `chmod +x`), then run it.

On first start it finds World of Warcraft: Forever by itself; if it can't, point it at your game folder.

## What it does

**Polestar's addons, kept up to date**
- Installs and updates **Polestar**, **Polestar Recorder** and **LibForeverData** straight from this project's GitHub releases.
- Every download is checked against the checksum published with it before your AddOns folder changes. Your other addons are never touched.
- Checks for updates on its own, or when you click **Check for updates**. You choose whether updates install automatically.

**Community guides in one click**
- On any community guide's page on [polestar.guide](https://polestar.guide), choose **Install with Polestar Client**. The app comes forward, shows what will be installed, and one click puts it in.
- The **Community Guides** page lists what you have: update, remove, or see what's new.
- Optional **automatic guide updates**, off by default. When on, routine updates install a day after they come out, while the game is closed. If an update has a new publisher, adds or removes folders, or the guide was replaced by a different one, it always asks you first.

**Careful with your PC**
- The list of community guides is signed by polestar.guide and checked by the app; a copy that was tampered with is refused.
- Only guide files go into AddOns, and only into the guide's own folders. Anything else in a download (other files, links pointing elsewhere) stops the install.
- If polestar.guide pulls a guide (broken, or taken down), the app turns it off or removes it and tells you why. A turned-off guide can be restored when the site allows it again.
- **Activity** lists every addon change on your PC, newest first.
- The app updates itself, and only accepts updates signed with Polestar's key.

**Recording, if you want to help (opt-in)**
- Turn on **sharing** and the app uploads what the Polestar Recorder addon logs while you play: quests, kills, NPC positions, trainers and vendors. It helps us build better routes and fix Forever's quest data.
- Character names are stripped before anything leaves your PC, and chat is never recorded. **What we send** shows you all of it. Pause or stop any time.
- One switch: turning sharing on in the app also turns the Recorder on in game. `/psrec off` in game still wins.
- Recording needs the new Recorder, which comes with **the next Polestar update**; until then the Status page says so.

**And the small things**
- A small window that lives in your tray when closed, and starts with your computer if you want it to.
- Works with WoW: Forever only.

## Good to know

- **You don't need Polestar Client to use Polestar.** CurseForge and the zips on GitHub keep working exactly as before.
- **No account, ever.** If you turn recording on, uploads carry a random ID for your install, nothing more.
- Report anything odd, from wording to crashes, on [Discord](https://discord.gg/vMbee6F2e9) or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar/issues). Screenshots help.

`latest.json` and the `.sig` files are for the app's built-in updater; you don't need them.
